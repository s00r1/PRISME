START WITH YOUR WATCH

Try PRISME
on your DM100.

  1. Export your current PRISME profile if you already use the app.
  2. Get PRISME-6.0.1.apk from the signed GitHub release and install it on your watch. Update over the existing version to preserve your data.
  3. Open PRISME, choose a desktop and grant only the permissions needed for the optional features you want.

No root or firmware replacement. Setting PRISME as the home app depends on your watch firmware.

BEFORE YOU INSTALL

A few useful details.

Which watches does it support?

The tested reference is a DM100 running full Android 7.1.1 at 640 × 480 landscape. PRISME targets large rectangular full-Android watches; other models and firmware variants need separate testing. Wear OS, watchOS, Tizen, round or square watches and smartphones are not validated targets.

Does it need an Internet connection?

The everyday tools work locally. Optional weather uses Open-Meteo after explicit consent and a manually selected city, without GPS. The provider receives the IP address and city query or city coordinates. PC Studio edits profiles locally in your browser.

Why does music information need a permission?

Optional track titles and artwork require Android notification access to reach media sessions. Simple playback controls remain available without that mode. The player must expose a compatible session; support for every player is not guaranteed.

What depends on the watch firmware?

Alarms, background playback, rotation and floating windows remain subject to Android and firmware limits. Some quick controls open Android settings. No quantified battery-life improvement or Play Protect certification is claimed.
