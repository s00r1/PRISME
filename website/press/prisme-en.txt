PRISME 6.0.1 — A new home screen for your DM100

PRISME is a free, MIT-licensed home screen and toolkit for large rectangular full-Android watches.

22 desktop styles, 13 widget types, optional weather, two control centers, floating volume and local notes, tasks and timers. Its browser-based PC editor lets you arrange a profile and import it on your watch.

Tested reference: DM100, Android 7.1.1, 640 x 480 landscape.
Minimum: full Android 7.1 / API 25.
No root or firmware replacement required.
Other models require separate validation. Wear OS, watchOS, Tizen and smartphones are not validated targets.

No advertising, account or telemetry. Notes, tasks and settings remain local. Optional weather uses Open-Meteo after explicit consent and manual city selection.
PC Studio and some detailed V6 screens are in French; English documentation is available.
