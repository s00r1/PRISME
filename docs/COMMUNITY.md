## English announcement

I’m sharing PRISME, a free open-source launcher for large rectangular full Android watches. The only device-tested reference is the DM100 (2.86-inch, 640 × 480, Android 7.1.1). It is not a Wear OS watch face and is not intended for round or square watches.

It includes 22 distinct layouts, a customizable iOS/Android-style Control Center, an expandable floating volume control, notes, checklist, timer, stopwatch and seven languages. No account or analytics in PRISME; MIT source and signed APK are available.

Website / screenshots: https://s00r1.github.io/PRISME/
APK and source: https://github.com/s00r1/PRISME

I’d appreciate feedback from DM100 owners on navigation, overlays, rotation and translations. Other rectangular full Android watches are unverified; please don’t assume compatibility from a similar model name. Known limitations are documented, including manufacturer battery restrictions and an unresolved lateral-display report.

If you test it, please share your model, Android version, resolution, PRISME version and what worked or failed. Please remove personal information from screenshots and don’t post device identifiers or account details. Happy to answer questions here.
